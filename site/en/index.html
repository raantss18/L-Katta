An assistant that knows when to keep out of the way

  • No window

    It lives on the edges of the screen and steps away from the window you're working in. It stays quiet during calls, hides behind full-screen videos and sleeps while you're away.

  • Free AI, no account

    It picks a free model for each question on its own. An OpenRouter key or a local Ollama add more.

  • Your email, calendar and terminal

    It sums up your email without marking it as read, reminds you before a meeting and runs commands in a Konsole tab you can see.

  • Nothing without your say-so

    An email is sent only after you've read the full text. Your keys stay in the desktop keyring; for sudo, you type the password yourself.

Meta + K opens its bubble, from anywhere.

Six lemurs, six personalities

Each one has its own gait and habits. In the app, type /species in its bubble to switch.
