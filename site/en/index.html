L Katta (Lemur Kata), from Madagascar to your desktop

A little lemur that lives on your Linux desktop.

L Katta knows your computer, helps without getting in the way, and wanders from one screen to another. Ask it a question, or ask for your email or your calendar: it answers in a speech bubble, then goes back to its own business.

What it does

In the spirit of the old Office paperclip, but useful and discreet. Everything fits in a single file: the lemur, its AI engine and the choice of models.

  • No window

    It lives right on your screen: it walks, climbs a vine, grows a tree and moves from one screen to another. Grab it and throw it: it lands on its feet.

  • Free AI, no account

    Its engine, ported from Tontolo, picks the best free model for each request on its own, and moves on to the next one when a model is busy. No key is required; an OpenRouter key or a local Ollama adds more models.

  • Email, calendar, contacts

    It sums up your unread email without marking it as read, reminds you before a meeting, and only sends an email after showing you the full text.

  • Your terminal

    It runs commands in a visible Konsole tab, reads what your terminal shows and types into it. For sudo, you always type the password yourself.

  • Strict rules

    Forbidden folders, forbidden commands, tools turned off: rules for the whole machine and for each account, enforced by the code, with the strictest one winning.

  • Discreet above all

    It stays near the edges and moves aside from the window you're working in. It hides when a video goes fullscreen, keeps quiet during video calls or in Do Not Disturb mode, and sleeps while you're away. When you come back, it sums up what it saved for later. One right-click, and it lets you be.

Meta+K opens its bubble, ready to type. Answers appear as they're written, with lists, code and clickable links, and it can find your past conversations (/historique). While you're chatting, it stays still.

Its settings live in its bubble (the gear icon): name, personality, bubbles, appearance, startup, email, API keys. It updates itself once you agree, and uses about 15 MB of memory.

Six lemurs to choose from

Each one has its own look and its own way of moving. In the app, type /especes in its bubble, then for example /regle espece sifaka: it changes in a little puff of cloud.

  • Ring-tailed lemur (maky): walks on all fours, striped tail held high; loves stretching in the sun.
  • Verreaux's sifaka: stands upright and hops sideways; it dances.
  • Indri (babakoto): the biggest one; leaps upright, and sings.
  • Aye-aye: slow, with big ears; it taps with its long finger.
  • Mouse lemur: tiny; it scurries in short bursts and keeps watch with its huge eyes.
  • Black-and-white ruffed lemur (varikandana): fluffy ruff, long black tail, loud calls.

Install L Katta

Download the AppImage, then open a terminal in the folder where the file is:

  1. Make it executablechmod +x {{APPIMAGE_NOM}}
  2. Install it: copies it to ~/.local/bin, adds a launcher, starts with your session./{{APPIMAGE_NOM}} installer
  3. Start itlkatta &

After that, it updates itself once you agree, or with lkatta maj.

What you need

  • 64-bit Linux (x86_64) with Wayland and layer-shell: KDE Plasma, Sway, Hyprland…
  • wl-clipboard for the clipboard.
  • For email: curl and secret-tool (libsecret).
  • A modest computer is enough: no local AI is required.

Your privacy

  • Your passwords and keys go into the desktop keyring, never into a file.
  • Nothing goes out without your OK: an email is sent only after you approve the full text, and any command that changes your system asks for confirmation.
  • No account: not with L Katta, and not with the free providers it uses by default.
  • Your sensitive folders (~/.ssh, keyrings…) are off-limits by default.

Good to know: when you ask it a question, the question goes to the AI provider you chose (a free one by default, or your own if you add a key). With a local Ollama, nothing leaves your computer. This site sets no cookies and contacts no outside service.
